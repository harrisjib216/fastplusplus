# Rejected claims

<sub>[fastpp](../README.md) › [Catalog](README.md)</sub>

43 candidates failed verification because the evidence itself was invalid: a benchmark that timed a no-op or a `sleep`, a hard-coded baseline column, a fast path that computed a different answer, numbers copied from design targets. Weak or first-party evidence is not a reason to reject; those entries stay in the catalog at a lower tier with their shortcomings written down.

Rejected candidates stay on `data/known_list.txt` so later searches skip them.
