2. RE-DERIVE THE EVIDENCE TIER from what you saw, not from the record:
   A — reproducible benchmark that runs against the named baseline: the
       benchmark code is public AND compiler, flags, and hardware (CPU
       model) are all stated. Who ran it does not matter: the project's
       own repo, a linked repo, a paper's artifact repo, or an independent
       third-party suite all qualify. Name the benchmark code path in
       tier_reason. Missing any of code/compiler/flags/CPU model: not A.
   B — peer-reviewed paper, or a well-known independent third-party
       benchmark suite, showing the win against the baseline, that falls
       short of A.
   C — first-party numbers that fall short of A, or numbers without
       methodology. The project's own benchmark code with partial
       methodology is C, not B.
   D — no numbers at all.
   Cap at C if the baseline was a debug/-O0 build, or flags are unstated and
   the gain looks implausible (>50x). For stdlib baselines, record which of
   libstdc++ / libc++ / MSVC STL were actually tested in baseline_stdlib.

   usability: "copyable" only with an OSI-approved license or a CC0-1.0 /

   no/unclear/other non-OSI license -> "reference"; licensed core with

   maintenance_status from last commit vs today: <=12 months "active",

   Correct any field that is wrong. Do not "correct" a field you could not
