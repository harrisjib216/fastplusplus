Candidates surfaced by verifiers and enrichers, not yet in any wave's records.
- Paper-access retiers (w10 batch 02): mochimazui/gpu-scanline-path-rendering (ACM TOG 35(6) 2016, DOI 10.1145/2980179.2982434) and NV_path_rendering (TOG 31(6) 2012, NVIDIA PDF) C to B when ACM or the PDFs are reachable.
- Paper-access lead (w10 raster gh-cpp2): arXiv 2603.23696 (CPU 2D rasterizer benchmark paper, blocked). ctx.graphics/performance tournament (ctx vs Blend2D, Qt6, Cairo, AGG on Cortex-A74) and tgfx.org/benchmark are blocked here. mfabbri77/VGCPU-Benchmark is a harness for Blend2D, Vello CPU, AGG, Skia, ThorVG, PlutoVG, Qt, Cairo, AmanithVG, Raqote with no published results yet: re-check later for a shootout source.
- Paper-access retiers (w10 video-audio): SVT-AV1 (SPIE 2020/2024 papers, AOM CTC results; D to B), uvgRTP (MMSP 2020; C to B), OpenVVC (arXiv 2205.12217, MMSys 2023), Kvazaar (SiPS 2015 on forth.gr), uavs3e (jdl.link PDF), Weissenberger and Schmidt GPU JPEG (arXiv 2111.09219), NeaTS-style snippet entries.
