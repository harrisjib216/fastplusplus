# Wave 10 leads for a seed pass

Candidates surfaced by verifiers and enrichers, not yet in any wave's records.
- Paper-access retiers (w10 batch 02): mochimazui/gpu-scanline-path-rendering (ACM TOG 35(6) 2016, DOI 10.1145/2980179.2982434) and NV_path_rendering (TOG 31(6) 2012, NVIDIA PDF) C to B when ACM or the PDFs are reachable.
- Paper-access lead (w10 raster gh-cpp2): arXiv 2603.23696 (CPU 2D rasterizer benchmark paper, blocked). ctx.graphics/performance tournament (ctx vs Blend2D, Qt6, Cairo, AGG on Cortex-A74) and tgfx.org/benchmark are blocked here. mfabbri77/VGCPU-Benchmark is a harness for Blend2D, Vello CPU, AGG, Skia, ThorVG, PlutoVG, Qt, Cairo, AmanithVG, Raqote with no published results yet: re-check later for a shootout source.
- Paper-access retiers (w10 video-audio): SVT-AV1 (SPIE 2020/2024 papers, AOM CTC results; D to B), uvgRTP (MMSP 2020; C to B), OpenVVC (arXiv 2205.12217, MMSys 2023), Kvazaar (SiPS 2015 on forth.gr), uavs3e (jdl.link PDF), Weissenberger and Schmidt GPU JPEG (arXiv 2111.09219), NeaTS-style snippet entries. All blocked here.

- Enricher lead (e05): google/skcms (Google's C++ colour management system, used by Skia and Chromium) is not catalogued; candidate for a color-and-resampling seed pass.
- Enricher leads (e07, image-codecs texture encoders): Traverse-Research/intel-tex-rs-2 (ISPC texture kernels sped up 2.1-6.2x over Intel ispc_texcomp, PR #50 merged 2026-09-28); richgel999/bc7enc_rdo (not catalogued; carries the bc7e.ispc determinism fix); QuickBC7 paper (Computers and Graphics 2026, ScienceDirect S0097849326001020, claims 2.23x versus etcpak 2.0; ScienceDirect blocked here).
